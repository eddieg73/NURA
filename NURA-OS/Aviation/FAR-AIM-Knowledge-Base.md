## International ops (Bahamas · Costa Rica · SCA — the founder's routes)
- eAPIS/CBP: manifest ≥60 min before departure (both directions); ICAO FPL format; passports + visas per country
- Bahamas: landing/cruising permits, BICA fees, customs airports list
- Costa Rica: overflight/landing permits (DGAC), fuel rules
- South/Central America: overflight permits per country (many require 24-72h advance), Spanish ATC basics, ICAO separation/QNH
- Fuel/currency: USD widely; landing fees vary; carry cash as backup
