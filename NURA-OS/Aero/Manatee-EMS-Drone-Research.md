## 1. Who operates 911 EMS in Manatee County

- **Manatee County EMS is a county-run "third service" provider** — a government-run EMS system that is *not* fire-department based. It is the primary 911 emergency response service for citizens and visitors. [V] https://www.mymanatee.org/departments/public-safety-department/emergency-medical-services-(ems)-division · https://www.governmentjobs.com/careers/manateecounty/jobs/newprint/4019996
- **Organizational home:** Public Safety Department, which also houses Emergency Communications (911), Emergency Management, Beach Patrol/Aquatic Safety, Community Health, and Marine Rescue. [V] https://www.mymanatee.org/departments/public-safety-department
- **Scale (2022):** 21+ ALS and BLS units (ambulances, firefighter-paramedics on fire apparatus, supervisors, community paramedics); 57,000+ calls/year; serves 403,000+ people across 748 sq mi and 150 mi of coastal waterways. [V] https://www.governmentjobs.com/careers/manateecounty/jobs/newprint/4019996 · Current volume ~60,000 medical calls/yr (2024-25). [V] https://www.wfla.com/news/local-news/manatee-county/manatee-county-could-expand-program-that-improves-response-times-for-emergency-vehicles/
- **Licensed by FL DOH** as an ALS EMS provider (license ALS4102, Manatee County Department of Public Safety). [V] https://mqa-internet.doh.state.fl.us/MQASearchServices/HealthcareProviders/LicenseVerificationPractitionerPrintFriendly?HasArnpAprnRelationship=False&LicInd=135&Procde=2601
- **911 PSAP:** the county Emergency Communications Center (ECC) is the main 911 call-taking center for all wireless callers countywide and landline callers outside the City of Bradenton. [V] https://www.mymanatee.org/departments/public-safety-department
- **Structure:** county EMS ambulances are co-located at many fire-district stations (some in county-owned stations); the county GIS layer distinguishes fire-only (F), fire-with-EMS (FE), stand-alone EMS (E), and the Myakka City fire station that supports a BLS unit (FM). [V] https://www.mymanatee.org/gisbads/rest/services/address/layers/MapServer/1
- **Accreditation:** CAAS-accredited (Commission on Accreditation of Ambulance Services) per the then-EMS chief (Nov 2024). [V] https://www.linkedin.com/posts/james-crutchfield-emschief_chief-emergency-medical-services-activity-7262638573860933632-4IXZ
- **Leadership:** Public Safety Director **Jodie Setnor Fiske** (confirmed by BCC after Jacob Saur departed July 2022). [V] https://thebradentontimes.com/stories/manatee-county-names-new-director-of-public-safety,43127 · **James Crutchfield** — EMS Chief Jul 2019–Dec 2024, now Deputy Director of Public Safety; EMS Chief role was being recruited as of late 2024 (current chief name **[U]**). ## 2. Fire/rescue districts and their roles

- **Eight fire districts/agencies** operate in Manatee County; six are independent special fire-control districts plus the City of Bradenton and City of Palmetto municipal departments. [V] https://www.mymanatee.org/services-and-amenities/service-listing/service-details/find-your-local-fire-department · https://www.mymanatee.org/gisps/rest/services/Fire/firedistricts/FeatureServer/0
- **Roles:** fire districts provide first-due fire/rescue and, increasingly, ALS first response; **county EMS is the sole ALS *transport* provider** (ambulance service). [V] https://resources.missioncriticalpartners.com/resources/mcp-assessment-helps-manatee-county-fl-ems-plan-its-future
- **Key districts:**
  - **West Manatee Fire & Rescue District** — independent special fire-control district covering **all three Anna Maria Island cities (Anna Maria, Bradenton Beach, Holmes Beach)** plus Cortez, Palma Sola, NW Bradenton; ~18 sq mi, pop. 27,733, **2.4M+ annual public-beach visitors**. [V] https://www.wmfr.org/about · Runs island ALS (first ALS engine at Station 3, Holmes Beach, 2018). [V] https://www.islander.org/2018/01/wmfr-aims-for-feb-1-for-als-startup/
  - **North River Fire District** — 82 sq mi; Ellenton, Gillette, Memphis, Palmetto, Parrish, Palm View, Piney Point, Rubonia, Snead Island, Terra Ceia; formed 1988. [V] https://www.nrfd.org/about-us
  - **Cedar Hammock, East Manatee, Southern Manatee, Myakka City** districts — special districts; East Manatee (Lakewood Ranch/Parrish corridor) absorbed the former Myakka City Fire Control District in Oct 2020. [V] https://www.mymanatee.org/gisps/rest/services/Fire/firedistricts/FeatureServer/0 · https://www.bradenton.com/news/local/article259392594.html
- **Historical integration model:** since 2001 county EMS stationed a county-paid "fire-medic" (dual firefighter/paramedic) at Myakka City Fire Control District to ride firetrucks; replaced by a full-time ALS ambulance at Myakka Station 1 (Wauchula Rd) in Oct 2020; program retired after East Manatee absorbed Myakka City. [V] https://www.yourobserver.com/news/2020/jun/24/hospitals-surge-to-help-ems-in-manatee-county/ · https://www.bradenton.com/news/local/article259392594.html
- **Takeaway:** fire districts are political stakeholders with their own elected boards and budgets — but EMS transport, dispatch integration, and medical oversight sit with the county. A drone program therefore needs the county (EMS + ECC) at the center and fire chiefs as coalition partners.

## 3. Documented response-time challenges

**Rural east county (the NURA Tier-1 sweet spot):**
- **Parrish:** before a dedicated crew was added, the primary ambulance sat at North River Fire Station (Ellenton) — routine response **~20 minutes**; backups 5.9–13.5 mi / 11–19 min away. County average then was 7.47 min (urban 6.74 vs **rural 10.87**, some rural >15 min — "Urban vs. Rural" model). [V] https://www.bradenton.com/news/local/health-care/article36401412.html · https://www.bradenton.com/news/local/health-care/article34900476.html
- **Myakka City / Lake Manatee pocket:** average response **~17 minutes** vs county average ~8 min, per Deputy Public Safety Director James Crutchfield; county moving to build a new EMS station near Lake Manatee because the existing one is dilapidated. [V] https://wslr.org/manatee-planning-commission-endorses-2000-more-homes-in-parrish/
- **Myakka City ALS history:** only one ALS ambulance east of I-75 in 2001; six added east of I-75 since then — growth is continuous (Parrish area growing "substantially"; east county is the growth corridor). [V] https://www.bradenton.com/news/local/article259392594.html · https://www.bradenton.com/news/local/health-care/article34900476.html
- **System strain:** surge events can leave only 6 of 21 ambulances available for 743 sq mi; EMS redistributes to hold ≤9 min response. [V] https://www.yourobserver.com/news/2020/jun/24/hospitals-surge-to-help-ems-in-manatee-county/ · Ambulance offload delay averaged 24.57 min. [V] https://www.bradenton.com/news/local/health-care/article34900476.html · 911 call volume grew **>70% FY2020→FY2023**. [V] https://www.snntv.com/manatee/every-second-counts-new-technology-in-ambulances-expected-to-improve-response-times-in-manatee-county/

**Beach zones (Anna Maria Island):**
- One ambulance based on the island + one in Cortez; **bridge/traffic congestion blocks mainland backup units** ("traffic congestion on the bridges hinders our response"). County tested a Marine Paramedic Unit (EMT/paramedic SUV on the island, 12-hr shifts) as a workaround. [V] https://www.fox13news.com/news/special-ems-patrol-planned-for-anna-maria-beaches
- A single crash can cut island access for hours (Manatee Ave bridge blocked both lanes, 2+ hrs, May 2026). [V] https://www.mysuncoast.com/2026/05/25/crash-snarls-traffic-cutting-off-north-access-anna-maria-island/
- Beach crowds: 2.4M visitors/yr; beach patrol handles 21 medical calls + 3 water rescues on a single July 4th (2025); lifeguarded zones only at Manatee Public Beach and Coquina Beach — the northern shoreline has **no lifeguard stations**. [V] https://www.islander.org/2026/06/1st-responders-gear-up-for-july-4/ · https://www.wmfr.org/about
- Mitigations already in play: Emergency Vehicle Preemption at 24 intersections (~11 sec saved/intersection; expansion to 93 proposed). [V] https://www.fox13news.com/news/new-technology-gives-green-light-manatee-county-ems-important-save-lives · https://www.wfla.com/news/local-news/manatee-county/manatee-county-could-expand-program-that-improves-response-times-for-emergency-vehicles/

## 4. Existing drone/UAS programs — Florida EMS and Manatee County

- **Manatee County hosts the FIRST-IN-THE-NATION 911-integrated emergency-response drone delivery program** (Manatee County + Archer First Response Systems + Tampa General Hospital), live since May 1, 2024 from the **EMS Lakewood Ranch Station (Malachite Drive)**. Delivers AED + Narcan (+ tourniquet) to 911 callers for cardiac arrest/overdose/trauma; **Phase 2 (Sept 29, 2025): 35 sq mi, 7 days/wk, 6am–10pm, equipment <3 min avg arrival**; ECC dispatchers deploy it and talk callers through use. [V] https://www.mymanatee.org/connect/news-and-information/news-and-information/article-detail/emergency-communications-911-division-posts/2025/09/24/drone-program-expands-to-35-square-miles--seven-days-a-week · https://www.tgh.org/news/tgh-press-releases/2024/april/tgh-manatee-county-archerfrs-introduce-first-drone-delivery-emergency-response-equipment
- **Platform & economics (the precedent NURA must beat/complement):** Freefly Systems **AltaX**, ASTM-certified parachute, ADS-B receiver, LTE modem, onboard computer; ground hub at the EMS station; "operates as a public aircraft"; county paid **$1/month as test site in year one, then $10,000/month turnkey** (drone+software+maintenance) with ArcherFRS contracted for piloting and maintenance. [V] https://www.yourobserver.com/news/2024/jul/07/drone-emergency-services-manatee-county/ · https://www.mymanatee.org/connect/news-and-information/news-and-information/article-detail/emergency-communications-911-division-posts/2025/09/24/drone-program-expands-to-35-square-miles--seven-days-a-week
- **Reality check on the incumbent:** through early Phase 2 the drone had been deployed only a handful of times (~1 call in the 3.5 sq mi era; one June 2024 OD response aborted for weather); hours capped by manpower (4 pilots, two 8-hr shifts); path to 24/7 is staffing, not approval. The program was still "defining the SOP for how a 911 dispatcher requests a drone" — i.e., **the playbook is young and the county wants more**. [V] https://www.yourobserver.com/news/2025/oct/07/manatee-county-ems-drone-expands/ · https://www.wusf.org/health-news-florida/2024-07-23/rescue-from-above-how-drones-may-narrow-emergency-response-times
- **FAA posture for this program:** approved to fly **BVLOS at ≤200 ft AGL with ADS-B receiver** under the FAA's updated public-safety drone regulations (April 2025). [V-as-reported] https://www.yourobserver.com/news/2025/oct/07/manatee-county-ems-drone-expands/
- **Manatee County Sheriff's Office UAS team:** established program (DJI fleet per 2018 Bard College data; **all pilots FAA Part 107 certified**; night-waiver as of 2017) for missing persons, accident/crime-scene documentation, SWAT, disaster recovery — plus a manned Aviation Unit. [V] https://www.manateesheriff.com/about/bureaus/enforcement/uas_team.php · https://www.atlasofsurveillance.org/a/FL0410000UAV · https://unmanned-aerial.com/drone-locates-lost-horseback-riders-for-florida-sheriffs-office
- **Florida first-movers to reference:**
  - **Lee County** — BCC approved 4 autonomous **Skydio X10** drones + docks (~$136K, Oct 2025) after using a docked drone to livestream Hurricane Milton damage to the EOC (2024); 7 drones total; seeking up to $100K FDLE reimbursement; also sole-sourced an ArcherFRS drone-med-delivery contract (June 2025). [V] https://www.leegov.com/publicsafety/News%20Documents/10-7-25%20Public%20Safety%20Drone%20Release.pdf · https://www.highergov.com/sl/contract-opportunity/fl-archer-frs-emergency-drone-systems-52819346/
  - **Lee County smart-AED network** (Avive, ~260 devices) as the adjacent access layer. [V] https://www.winknews.com/wink_investigations/in-a-heartbeat-how-lee-county-is-trying-to-rewrite-the-odds-for-cardiac-arrest/article_7fe7cf04-7d7b-4108-96d5-9446b65f0ab0.html
  - **National clinical validation:** AED-drone pilot studies in Forsyth County NC / James City County VA / Clemmons NC funded by a 4-year American Heart Association grant — useful as clinical evidence citations for a county pitch. [V] https://www.wusf.org/health-news-florida/2024-07-23/rescue-from-above-how-drones-may-narrow-emergency-response-times

## 5. Airspace & regulatory context

**Airspace:**
- **SRQ (Sarasota-Bradenton International) is Class C airspace** (tower 0600–0000, Tampa Approach) — drone ops in/near it require prior ATC authorization via **LAANC or DroneZone (Part 107.41)**; SRQ is ~10 NM from central Bradenton, so the western county (Palmetto/Bradenton) and AMI beach zones are inside or near the shelf — check current sectional + UAS Facility Maps before route design. [V] https://www.faa.gov/flight_deck/pilot_info/srq.pdf · https://www.airnav.com/airport/KSRQ · https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/foa_html/chap19_section_6.html
- Neighbors: **MacDill AFB 27 NM north** (Tampa restricted airspace), **Venice Muni 20 NM south**, Tampa Class B farther north. [V] https://www.airnav.com/airport/KSRQ · East county (Myakka City, Parrish) is largely **Class G/Class E** — the most permissive rural corridor. [V] https://www.airnav.com/airport/KSRQ
- **Florida State Parks: launching/landing drones is prohibited** (Fla. Admin. Code 62D-2.014(15)) **except in emergency when human life is endangered** — Lake Manatee State Park (county), Myakka River State Park (adjacent Sarasota), Terra Ceia, DeSoto NM context: emergency medevac drops OK, routine ops need DEP permission. [V] https://www.floridastateparks.org/plan-your-visit/faqs · https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/florida/Fla-Admin-Code-Ann-R-62D-2-014
- Beaches/over-water: AMI and the 150-mi coastline are open-airspace but crowded-people environments (Part 107.39 over-people limits) and bird/wildlife sensitivities (state-park rationale applies to county preserves such as Robinson Preserve) — plan shoreline corridors and beach landings carefully. [V] https://www.floridastateparks.org/plan-your-visit/faqs

**FAA framework:**
- Baseline: **Part 107** (commercial sUAS, <55 lb, VLOS, 400 ft AGL, Remote ID, LAANC authorizations); waivers for night (107.29), over-people (107.39), BVLOS (107.31) via DroneZone. [V] https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/foa_html/chap19_section_6.html
- **April 2025 FAA public-safety rule update** enables public-safety BVLOS at ≤200 ft AGL with ADS-B receive capability — the basis of the Manatee/ArcherFRS operations. [V-as-reported] https://www.yourobserver.com/news/2025/oct/07/manatee-county-ems-drone-expands/
- **National BVLOS rulemaking in progress:** FAA/TSA BVLOS NPRM published Aug 7, 2025 (proposed **Part 108**, permit/certificate tiers, UTM under Part 146; mandated by FAA Reauthorization Act of 2024 §930; EO "Unleashing American Drone Dominance" June 2025 pushes a final rule ~240 days); comment period reopened Feb 2026. A permanent BVLOS lane is expected — NURA's waiver stack should track it. [V] https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2025-08-07/pdf/2025-14992.pdf · https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/full_text/html/2025/06/11/2025-10814.html · https://thefederalregister.org/documents/2026-01644/normalizing-unmanned-aircraft-systems-beyond-visual-line-of-sight-operations-reopening-of-comment-period
- **Public-aircraft ops:** the county's ArcherFRS drone "operates as a public aircraft" — the public-aircraft/government-contractor structure is the proven local model for medical payload delivery; legal structure should be confirmed with aviation counsel. **[U on details]**

**Florida law (state preemption is NURA's friend):**
- **F.S. 330.41:** authority to regulate UAS operation is vested in the **state**; political subdivisions (counties/cities) **may not** enact ordinances on UAS design, operation, airspace, altitude, or flight paths, and may not block drone-port siting. No county-by-county drone rules — one state + FAA regime. [V] https://flsenate.gov/Laws/statutes/2024/330.41
- **F.S. 934.50:** law-enforcement drone use requires a warrant except emergencies (incl. **imminent danger to life**); surveillance-of-private-property restrictions; **all government agencies must use drones from the FL Department of Management Services' approved-manufacturer list** (foreign/non-compliant platforms like DJI phased out by Jan 1, 2023); **FDLE drone grant program** reimburses agencies for compliant replacements, prioritizing rural counties (provision expires July 1, 2026). [V] https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2025/934.50
- **Federal funding constraint:** the **American Security Drone Act of 2023 (NDAA FY2024 §1825)** bans use of federal grant funds for drones from covered foreign entities from Dec 22, 2025 — **any federal-funded NURA fleet must be non-covered/US-built (e.g., Skydio-class)**. [V] https://www.fema.gov/sites/default/files/documents/fema_gpd_hsgp-nofo-fy2026_0.pdf

## 7. SWOT

**Strengths (opportunity side):**
- County is a proven drone-EMS innovator: first-in-nation 911-integrated program, commission-championed, SOPs already partially written.
- Documented, quantified pain points (17-min rural response; AMI bridge vulnerability; 70% call-volume growth) that map 1:1 to NURA's Tier-1 value prop.
- State preemption (330.41) = no county-by-county ordinance maze; FAA BVLOS public-safety rule + Part 108 NPRM = regulatory tailwind.
- Existing infrastructure to plug into: ECC/CAD, EMS stations, MCSO Part-107 team, CAAS-accredited EMS.
- 934.50 "imminent danger to life" exception + state-park emergency exception support medical-response flights.

**Weaknesses:**
- Incumbent vendor (ArcherFRS/TGH) owns the "911 drone" narrative and a $10K/mo contract; differentiation must be demonstrated, not claimed.
- County procurement = competitive/sole-source process; NURA has no verified county contract history.
- Payload pods (Rx, blood, antivenom) need medical direction + pharmacy-board compliance — more complex than AED/Narcan over-the-counter payloads.
- NURA's EMS agency is still "in formation" (FL DOH licensure package) — no operational EMS license yet.

**Opportunities:**
- ArcherFRS coverage is one fixed site (Lakewood Ranch) — **the rural east and AMI zones are unserved**; ambulance-mounted mobile doctrine is a structurally different capability.
- Blood/anti-venom cold-chain pods, EMD telepresence, and TAK/Lattice integration = capabilities no Florida agency operates today.
- Funding stack is real: HSGP/SHSP + FDLE grant + AHA + hospital sponsorship; east-county rural priority fits the FDLE rural-first rule.
- FAA Part 108 final rule (expected within ~240 days of June 2025 EO) will normalize BVLOS — first-mover county positioning compounds.

**Threats:**
- FAA Part 108 final rule could shift waiver requirements mid-build; SRQ Class C + MacDill + beach crowding constrain routes (west county harder than east).
- American Security Drone Act + FL DMS approved-manufacturer list lock out DJI-class hardware in the government lane — supply-chain and cost implications.
- Privacy/political sensitivity (934.50 surveillance optics; county charter debate adds political churn 2026).
- ArcherFRS/TGH could expand to 24/7 and into rural zones before NURA reaches the county; hospital incumbency is sticky.

## 8. Recommended entry strategy (3 bullets)

1. **Enter as the "mobile/rural/clinical" complement, not the AED competitor:** pitch the BCC + Public Safety leadership (Fiske/Crutchfield) a Phase-3 expansion concept — ambulance-roof-mounted Tier-1 drones serving the east-county 15–20-min response pockets (Myakka City, Parrish, Lake Manatee) and AMI bridge-isolated zones, with EMD telepresence and cold-chain blood/anti-venom pods under county medical direction. Anchor the ask in the county's own numbers (17-min Lake Manatee average; AMI bridge closures) and position it as extending — not displacing — the ArcherFRS program.
2. **Lead with a no-cost/at-risk pilot in the ArcherFRS mold:** mirror the proven county template — commission-approved pilot MOU ($1-scale initial year), hospital co-sponsor (TGH for scale, Manatee Memorial/Sarasota Memorial for trauma), ECC/CAD integration at one station, FAA BVLOS ≤200-ft public-safety authorization, phased coverage. Deliverables: response-time delta study vs. ground units (AHA-style metrics) plus cold-chain/pod SOPs the county lacks today.
3. **Stack funding before asking for county dollars:** pursue FDLE drone-grant reimbursement (rural-priority, expires 7/1/2026 — act now), FEMA SHSP via FDEM (AEL 03OE-07-SUAS; ASDA-compliant airframes only), AHA research funding, and a hospital sponsorship lane; use county general-fund dollars as the last tranche. Meanwhile, credential the NURA EMS agency (FL DOH licensure) and complete the Part 107 → BVLOS waiver stack so the county is never asked to carry regulatory risk.
