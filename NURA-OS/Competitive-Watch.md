## Brief — 2026-08-28

### 3 Competitor Moves

1. **Microsoft Dragon Copilot opened AI Apps & Agents GA on Microsoft Marketplace (Aug 21, 2026).** Partners (Canary Speech, Humata Health, Optum, Regard) can now build/validate/certify/publish; organizations buy/deploy inside the Dragon Admin Center. Also: UpToDate integration lets Dragon cite evidence; **May 1, 2026 pricing restructure** killed the short-lived ~$150-160 "Physician Practice" tier and moved everyone to a single reduced-price per-user license + per-encounter AI consumption fee; 60% rural-hospital discount via Pivot Point Consulting. (Microsoft Blog / GIXtools / Plaud review)
2. **Epic shipped native AI Charting (Feb 2026).** Platform-native ambient scribe (part of "Art") that drafts notes + suggested orders; Epic holds 42% acute hospital EHR market / 55% beds. Rumored ~$80/provider/mo. Sherlocks "scribe-only" point solutions (Abridge, Ambience, Nabla); forces differentiation into complex specialties, multi-EHR, and revenue capture. (STAT / HitConsultant / MedTech Dive)
3. **Suki published KLAS-validated ROI.** Across FMOL Health, McLeod Health, Rush: avg incremental revenue **+$1,223/provider/mo**, 35-65% reduction in after-hours documentation; McLeod -27% doc time (+$1,004/mo); FMOL +6.5% level-4 visits (+$862/mo). (Fierce Healthcare / KLAS)

Running context: Abridge = 2026 Best in KLAS ambient speech (94.7); Ambience $243M Series C @$1.25B + Cleveland-Clinic 4,000-clinician deployment.

### 2 Opportunities for NURA

1. **Epic went native → "scribe-only" is dead; the surviving wedge is the EMR-agnostic AI operating layer.** NURA is born-agnostic (per-EMR adapters) and ships coding + care-gap + prior-auth + decision support, not just a note. A platform-native Epic tool can't serve a multi-EHR practice; NURA can. Lead with the AI operating layer, not the scribe.
2. **The ROI bar just moved.** Competitors now publish KLAS revenue deltas (+$1,223/mo). NURA must meet the same evidence bar: publish Medisun/North Miami reference outcomes and complete the MIMIC 10K + RCT validation + SOC2/HITRUST in flight.
