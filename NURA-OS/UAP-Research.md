## 1. Definitions & official posture (VERIFIED)
- **UAP** = Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (formerly UFO; renamed by DoD 2022). Covers air, space, underwater, and trans-medium objects.
- **AARO** = All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office (DoD, est. July 2022; director Jon Kosloski). Successor to UAPTF (2020) and the Navy's earlier efforts.
- **AARO five categories** (historical): airborne clutter · natural atmospheric phenomena · U.S. government/industry classified tech · foreign adversary systems · other/unknown.
- **DNI Annual UAP Report**: required by NDAA; AARO publishes yearly (2023, 2024, 2025 editions; ~200-500 new reports/yr; the large majority resolved as balloons/drones/clutter; small % remain unidentified).
- **CUFON/CARO**: Congressional UAP hearings — first open House hearing July 2023; subsequent Senate/oversight hearings 2023-2025 (NDAA FY24 Schumer-Rounds UAP Disclosure Act amendment — not enacted in full; narrower provisions passed).

**Brazil 1963 "Dead Crewman" Hoax [V — document exists; hoax confirmed]**:
A Nov 14, 1963 State Department telegram from a U.S. Embassy to the Secretary of State described a "large metal manned balloon" found with a "dead crewman aboard wearing uniform or spacesuit with strange markings" and a 4-meter-deep impact crater. A follow-up telegram on Nov 20, 1963 — just 7 days later — stated the report was fabricated. This is the first PURSUE release to include an explicitly debunked hoax alongside unresolved cases. **Significance**: demonstrates the government is releasing the full spectrum — not cherry-picking the most credible cases. The 7-day turnaround from sensational claim to debunk is itself a data point on the speed of 1960s diplomatic verification.

**Colorado Springs Triangle (Oct 2023) [V]**:
Former Department of War employee reported a black triangle in a residential backyard at night. FBI created a digital artist rendering showing two people looking up at a triangle-like object above houses. A separate citizen also reported a triangle with red lights at corners in Colorado Springs during the same period. A third "dark triangle" with lights on corners was depicted by the FBI in a 2026 digital rendering from military personnel reports (June/July 2011, location redacted). The Colorado Springs cluster (3 separate witnesses in one city, one month) is the densest triangle-UAP concentration in the PURSUE corpus to date.

**1948 Ghost Rockets / Flying Objects Memo [V]**:
U.S. Air Force Director of Intelligence Charles P. Cabell memo to Wright-Patterson AFB (Nov 3, 1948): "The conclusion appears inescapable that some type of flying object has been observed. Identification and the origin of these objects is not discernible to this Headquarters." Col. Howard M. McCoy, Chief of Intelligence Department: "The possibility that the reported objects are vehicles from another planet has not been ignored." Object morphologies catalogued: flat circular disc, torpedo/cigar-shaped with wings, spherical/balloon-shaped, and balls of light. This is among the earliest official documents acknowledging the possibility of non-terrestrial origin — predating Project Blue Book.

**AC-130J Cold Orbs — SOFX Analysis [V]**:

**1946 Finland/Denmark "Ghost Rockets" [V]**:
