# RATCHET — THE AMOS EXPANSION (the Autonomous Medical Operating System · 2026-08-04, founder canonical)

**Ratchet is evolving beyond a medical robot into an AUTONOMOUS MEDICAL OPERATING SYSTEM (AMOS)** — the medical OS for Earth, Antarctica, the Moon, and Mars.

## THE STACK (the four-layer vision)
```
Hermes      = the OS + orchestration kernel for the ENTIRE NURA ecosystem
Paperclip   = enterprise operations + business management
NURA Clinical = the healthcare platform, EMR, clinical workflows
RATCHET     = autonomous medical intelligence + robotic clinical operations (the AMOS)
```

## THE 18 MAJOR CAPABILITIES
1. **Medical Digital Twin** — every patient's continuously updated twin: history · genomics/pharmacogenomics (when available) · wearable data · lab trends · imaging · meds · functional status · environmental exposures · behavioral health · baselines. **Ratchet compares against the INDIVIDUAL's baseline, not population averages.**
2. **Hospital Digital Twin** — every clinic/field hospital/spacecraft/station: equipment · med inventory · blood · oxygen · staffing · ORs · vehicles · battery/generator · water · HVAC · sterilization. **Ratchet always knows what's ACTUALLY available.**
3. **Autonomous Laboratory** — operates/coordinates: chemistry · CBC · PCR · microbiology · digital pathology · microscopy · blood typing · blood gases · toxicology — choosing the highest-value tests while conserving reagents.
4. **Autonomous Radiology** — Orthanc/OHIF/ThaiRIS/DICOM/DICOMweb · X-ray/US/CT/MRI/PET/nuclear/echo/retinal/derm — the workflow: **AI preliminary interpretation → differential → confidence → radiologist review → final report** (the NURA Rad doctrine).
5. **Autonomous Pharmacy** — inventory forecasting · interactions · compounding · automated dispensing · cold-chain monitoring · expiration prediction · therapeutic substitution · antimicrobial stewardship.
6. **Medical Manufacturing** — 3D printing: splints · surgical guides · prosthetics · dental appliances · replacement parts · orthotics · anatomical models; longer-term: bioprinting · custom implants · personalized drug production.
7. **Surgical Navigation** — NOT autonomous surgery initially: AR · anatomy recognition · instrument tracking · US/CT/MRI fusion · bleeding prediction · safety boundaries — **the "GPS for surgery."**
8. **Biomedical Engineering Assistant** — troubleshoot: ventilators · US machines · ECGs · infusion pumps · defibrillators · anesthesia machines · analyzers · sterilizers — maintenance schedules + guided repairs (no bio-med engineer on site).
9. **Infectious Disease Intelligence** — the continuous epidemiology engine: outbreaks · antibiotic resistance · contact tracing · isolation recommendations · vaccination status · environmental contamination · wastewater surveillance.
10. **Disaster Command Center** — the medical incident commander: coordinates ambulances · drones · hospitals · blood banks · air transport · ICU beds · ORs · personnel · supply chains — continuously updating triage priorities.
11. **Space Physiology Laboratory** — dedicated models: microgravity · lunar/Martian gravity · radiation · bone loss · muscle atrophy · cardiovascular adaptation · vision changes · immune function · circadian rhythm.
12. **Preventive Medicine Engine** — predicts instead of reacting: sepsis · HF deterioration · stroke risk · diabetes progression · kidney injury · mental-health decline · falls · frailty.
13. **Genomic Medicine** — rare diseases · cancer genomics · pharmacogenomics · carrier screening · precision medicine · gene-therapy planning.
14. **Environmental Health** — air/water quality · mold · radiation · noise · chemical exposure · temp/humidity · CO · CO2 · VOCs.
15. **Rehabilitation Robot** — PT · OT · speech therapy · gait analysis · balance testing · strength assessment · home-exercise monitoring.
16. **Medical Education System** — the always-available instructor: EMTs · nurses · PAs · physicians · dentists · pharmacists · respiratory therapists · lab personnel · students — simulation · virtual patients · competency tracking.
17. **Federated Learning Network** — sites share model improvements + validated insights WITHOUT sharing raw patient data (privacy + governance preserved).
18. **Planetary Health Network** — every deployment connected: rural hospitals · Antarctica · offshore platforms · cruise ships · military bases · disaster zones · lunar habitats · Mars settlements — each learns from validated cases, updates reviewed + SIGNED before deployment.
