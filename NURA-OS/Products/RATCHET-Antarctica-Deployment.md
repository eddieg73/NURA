# RATCHET ANTARCTICA — THE FIRST REAL-WORLD DEPLOYMENT (2026-08-04, founder canonical)

**The closest operational bridge between terrestrial austere medicine and lunar/Martian medical autonomy.** Antarctic stations already reproduce: prolonged isolation · delayed/impossible evacuation · limited specialists · one physician or a tiny team · nonmedical trained assistants · constrained pharmacy/lab/imaging/surgery · dangerous weather + transport windows · equipment failure far from spares. (Australian stations: inaccessible up to 9 months winter — doctors depend on trained lay surgical assistants + remote telemedicine. BAS: one physician at Rothera in winter.)

## CORE OPERATING ROLES
1. **Station clinical copilot:** triage + differentials · longitudinal records · med/allergy checks · ECG/US assist · lab interpretation · procedural guidance · dental emergencies · behavioral monitoring · documentation · teleconsult packets · reassessment reminders. Displays separately: confirmed findings · suspected diagnoses · must-not-miss · immediate actions · available vs ideal treatment · evacuation threshold · confidence/uncertainty.
2. **DOCTOR-INCAPACITATION MODE — the Sole Clinician Down Protocol** (wintering physicians have historically needed appendectomies with ONE doctor on station): detect physician illness/injury → alert leadership → identify best-qualified responder → open the physician's emergency record → establish telemedicine → assign trained lay assistants → inventory meds/equipment → guided assessment → escalate per DETERMINISTIC protocol → prolonged on-site management → continuous evacuation feasibility. The system knows EXACTLY who has: IV/IO access · airway · med prep · sterile technique · suturing · US · splinting · dental assist · OR support · prolonged monitoring.
3. **Lay surgical assistant mode:** "You are Assistant Two. Maintain sterile retraction at the marked position. Do not advance deeper than the visual boundary. Tell me immediately if bleeding increases or the physician loses responsiveness." The interface shows ONLY what that assistant needs — never a surgical textbook.
4. **Remote field-medic mode** (parties hours/days away): rugged tablet · offline models · sat/radio · compact US · ECG · pulse ox · capnography · thermometer · environmental sensors · med inventory · AR/video guidance · low-power mode. Covers: frostbite · hypothermia · snow blindness · trauma · crevasse falls · crush · altitude illness · CO exposure · burns · dehydration · equipment injury · prolonged casualty care · improvised-shelter medicine.

**Cold injury:** mild/moderate/severe hypothermia · frostnip/frostbite · nonfreezing cold injury · afterdrop · cold diuresis · arrhythmia risk · rewarming methods · transport protection · thaw-when-refreezing-possible decisions. Integrates: core temp · ambient · wind · wet clothing · exposure time · mental status · rhythm · shelter/transport.
**Environmental/occupational:** CO · CO2 · fuel/solvent · generator exhaust · fire/smoke · pressure/ventilation failure · UV · cold-air dehydration · sleep/circadian · machinery injuries · aircraft/vehicle incidents.
**High-altitude physiology** (South Pole = hypobaric hypoxia + isolation — the NASA-relevant analog): altitude-adjusted baselines for SpO2 · HR · sleep · cognition · exertional capacity · hematocrit · AMS symptoms.
**Behavioral health** (winter-over confinement/darkness — Palmer Station = NASA's spaceflight analog): private check-ins · sleep/fatigue tracking · cognitive testing · depression/anxiety screening · conflict de-escalation · suicide-risk escalation · workload/rest recommendations · confidential telebehavioral. **Never workplace surveillance.**
**Evacuation intelligence:** continuously evaluates severity × expected deterioration × local capacity × weather × aircraft/vessel availability × runway/sea-ice × transport duration/medical requirements × risk to rescuers → manage locally · begin consultation · prepare evacuation · urgent evacuation when feasible · **evacuation presently impossible — activate prolonged-care plan.** Never falsely imply evacuation exists.
