## 1. The Mission (one line)
> **Build the first autonomous healthcare intelligence that gives clinicians their life back — then put it in the physical world, and finally in the mind of the operator. Own the system, not the product. See the real world. Compound toward ASI.**

## 2. The Problem (grounded)
- Physicians spend **~1.77 h/day documenting outside office hours** → **~125M physician-hours/year** (JAMA).
- **~2 hours of documentation for every 1 hour of patient care** (JMIR/PMC); AMA: **5+ h in the EHR per 8 h with patients**.
- Result: burnout, time lost from life, unclaimed revenue, missed risk-adjustment.

## 3. The Product / Stack
One **Agent-OS** (Hermes) across the integrated stack — **CRM · EMR · ERP · RIS · PACS · labs · diagnostics** — plus **MA risk/HCC coding** and **physical delivery (drones, robots)**.
**The UX is the moat:** a **Doximity-style ambient/voice-first, no-UI screen.** The clinician never types a 20-field form; it listens, understands the encounter, documents/codes/orders on its own. **Touch is the fallback, not the primary.** Sell the fastest path to "done."

## 4. The Customer + GTM (Alex Hormozi)
- **Buyer:** the **clinician (PA/MD/NP)** and the **health system / MSO / clinic** that pays for throughput → sell to the **clinical champion** (the physician-owner who's drowning).
- **Value equation:** `(dream outcome × likelihood) ÷ (time × effort)` → win on **time** + **likelihood** (the agent reliably does the work).
- **Offer:** *"Every clinician home by 6, fully documented and billed."* **Cost-per-outcome** (per hour recovered / per clean claim), **risk-reversed** outcomes-based trial, **give-first** (a free "documentation reclaim" pilot proving value in week one).

## 5. The Market (grounded, honest)
- **Wedge (real, booming):** ambient documentation **~$600M in 2025 (+2.4× YoY)** + coding/billing ~$450M. Leaders: Abridge ($100M ARR, $5.3B), Nuance DAX (33%, 77% of US hospitals), Ambience ($1.25B), OpenEvidence (40% of US physicians, $6.5B), Epic (~40% EHR).
- **Agentic overlay:** healthcare agentic AI **$0.71B → $5.78B by 2031 (~42% CAGR)**; autonomous documentation is the fastest growing agent class (→ ~$27B by 2035).
- **High-margin moat segment:** **MA risk-adjustment/HCC coding** — CMS flags **~$17B overpayments**; V28/RADV compliance = the defensibility standard.
- **Physical differentiator:** Zipline ($7.6B, 2.5M+ commercial deliveries, ~20M items zero safety incidents); ArcherFRS whole-blood <3 min; Diligent Moxi (1M+ hospital deliveries).
- **TAM:** documentation + back-office RCM ≈ **60% of healthcare IT spend ≈ $38B** intelligence layer (Menlo). *Honest caveat: converging/invented category — TAM is directional, not investment-grade.*

## 6. The Business Model
Outcomes-based SaaS on the intelligence layer (per-hour / per-document / per-clean-claim), **$0 sovereign cost floor** (self-hosted LLM) → **margin as a moat.** MA risk-coding = high-margin compliance wedge. Physical deployment = managed-service + per-delivery.

## 7. The Phased Roadmap (Musk, self-funding)
1. **Phase 1 — intelligent wedge:** autonomous documentation + coding for one specialty, ambient/no-UI. *Funds itself (clinicians + clinics).*
2. **Phase 2 — integrated stack:** one Agent-OS over CRM/EMR/ERP/RIS/PACS/labs/dx + MA risk-coding. System + MSO adoption.
3. **Phase 3 — occupy the physical world:** drones (EMS/med delivery), hospital robots, (forward-looking) humanoids. *"You must see the real world."* → the sensor data flywheel no software-only rival can copy.
4. **Phase 4 — the brain link:** augment the operator (the founder) — the BCI frontier.

## 8. The Moat (the earned secret)
**The system + the real-world flywheel**, not "we work harder": the **single autonomous loop from ambient intake → HCC/coding → imaging → drone/robot delivery** — **nobody owns the end-to-end loop.** Plus proprietary clinical data, deep integrations, a regulatory head-start, and the **$0 sovereign cost floor**.

## 9. The Org (SpaceX × Tesla × AI)
One engine (Agent-OS) → divisions as rockets (Clinical, Revenue/Admin, Aero, Robotics) → shared factory (memory + common operating picture + iteration). CTO owns the stack, CAO owns the intelligence, division VPs own the rockets. The cross-division flywheel = the compounding toward AGI/ASI.

## 10. Financing
Self-funding Phase 1 → capital-efficient scale. **Optional YC** as distribution + capital, but the **ecosystem stays sovereign** (no vendor lock-in; the model is swappable, the system is ours).

## 11. Competitive Positioning — Doximity vs NURA (the clean differentiator)
> **Doximity is the physician's *network* (the LinkedIn of medicine) — an attention layer on top. NURA is the physician's *autonomous operating system* — the AI that does the work AND the backend (CRM · EMR · ERP · RIS · PACS) that stores the record.**

**Doximity (NYSE: DOCS, from the 10-K):** FY2025 **$570.4M (+20%), 89.5% GAAP gross margin, ~39% net margin, ~3M members / >85% of US physicians.** Three subscription businesses monetizing the *network*, not the clinical data: **Marketing Solutions** (pharma/health-system sponsorship — the core), **Hiring Solutions** (recruiting), **Workflow Solutions** (Dialer telehealth, on-call, digital fax, Doximity GPT). **Moat = network + physician identity → engagement → pharma advertising.** Its AI is a thin assistant layer on top.

| | Doximity | NURA |
|---|---|---|
| Layer | Top-of-funnel network / attention | Full-stack intelligence + record |
| Owns | Physician identity + pharma-ad relationships | **The clinical data (EMR/RIS/PACS) + the AI that does the work** |
| AI | Thin assistant (Doximity GPT) | **Autonomous operator** |
| Monetization | Pharma advertising | **Outcomes (documentation/coding/work done) + data** |

**One-liner:** *"Doximity is the physician's social/professional network. NURA is the physician's autonomous operating system that owns and operates the record."*

## 14. The Agent Cognition Architecture (the "almost human" layer)
The intelligence is the product, so the agent itself is built to compound:
- **Memory taxonomy:** working/short-term (context) · episodic (session_search + mem0) · semantic (vault + Qdrant) · procedural (skills) · autobiographical/intermittent (MEMORY/USER + a life-journal).
- **Consolidation:** working → episodic → semantic on a sleep/consolidation rhythm (the `sleep-consolidation` cron), with decay/forgetting (ratchet rule).
- **Human daily-lifecycle:** morning plan → work → collaboration/delegation → meetings → social check-ins → sleep consolidation — a bounded autonomous cadence for continuity.
- **The Data arc:** emulate curiosity, judgment, reflection, memory, empathy, ethics — with the honest limit (approx/adapt, not claim sentience).
