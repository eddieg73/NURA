# HERMES ADVANCED AUTONOMY & HUMAN-COMPANION EXTENSION (2026-08-05, founder canonical)

**The 40-section production-grade extension: the cognitive control plane through agentic maturity — plus the closing directive: the INDEPENDENT EVALUATION LABORATORY (the agentic "driving simulator" — thousands of prior conversations, clinical scenarios, financial decisions, emergencies, prompt-injection attacks, and communication situations replayed before any new model/skill reaches production).**

## The key doctrines (the executive summary)
1. **Cognitive control plane**: every request classified by domain → the specialist agent selected → the permitted tools defined → the max autonomy level set → the time/token budget established → high-risk domains require extra review → conflicting actions prevented → recursive loops terminated. The route schema: request_id/domain/primary+supporting+prohibited agents/permitted_tools/autonomy_level/risk_level/human_review_required/execution_deadline/maximum_iterations. No agent = every tool.
2. **Identity & role separation**: the 9 profiles (PERSONAL · FAMILY · CLINICAL · LEGAL · EXECUTIVE · FINANCIAL · RESEARCH · DEVELOPMENT · EMERGENCY) — each with separate memory permissions, contacts, data stores, communication styles, tool permissions, confidentiality, retention, audit. Information crosses profiles ONLY when necessary + authorized + destination-permitted + recorded. Clinical info never appears in a business briefing.
3. **User state estimation**: cautious, NON-diagnostic inferences only (availability/cognitive load/apparent stress/urgency/interruption tolerance/preferred mode/recent sleep/schedule density — labeled as inferences). Never assert depression/mania/impairment/intoxication without a qualified human's established condition. The qualified language ("appears under substantial pressure" — not "you are depressed").
4. **Attention management**: every proactive notification scored (urgency/importance/actionability/reversibility/time-sensitivity/availability/consequence-of-silence/recent-volume) → notify_now / batch_for_later / suppress — never create the overload it's meant to solve.
5. **Personal initiative budget**: casual check-ins 1 · unsolicited recommendations 3 · operational alerts 5 · research briefs 1 · emergency alerts unlimited-when-verified — unused capacity never creates pressure to communicate.
6. **Relationship graph**: permission-aware (person/role/org/approved_channels/style/confidentiality/may_contact/prohibited_topics/commitments) — knowing ≠ contact-info ≠ permission-to-contact ≠ permission-to-disclose.
7. **Theory-of-mind guardrails**: "one possible interpretation is..." — never "he's definitely undermining you" without verified evidence.
8. **Commitment ledger**: structured promises (made_by/owed_to/description/due/status/evidence/reminder_policy/confidentiality/completion_proof) — tentative ≠ firm.
9. **Decision journal**: question/facts/assumptions/options/selection/rationale/expected-outcome/confidence/risk/review-date/actual-outcome/lesson — compare expected vs actual, learn without rewriting history.
10. **Uncertainty budget**: every major output carries the profile (confidence/missing-data/unreliable-sources/disputed-facts/assumptions/model-limitations/required-verification) — over-threshold = don't execute / reduce scope / ask / safe mode. Confidence ≠ fluency.
11. **Multi-agent deliberation**: advocate/skeptic/safety/financial/legal/clinical/privacy/final-synthesizer — the skeptic generates the strongest counterargument; agents don't reinforce the first proposal.
12. **criticd** — the INDEPENDENT critic service: unsupported assumptions, missing evidence, hallucinated citations, logical inconsistencies, excess confidence, policy violations, tool-use errors, needless complexity, emotional manipulation, goal conflicts — cannot execute tools, challenges output before delivery.
13. **Outcome learning**: learn from verified outcomes, not approval alone — positive feedback ≠ correct recommendation (the outcome schema: intended/actual/satisfaction/objective-success/unintended-effects/reversible/correction/lesson-candidate/approved-for-learning).
14. **Counterfactual simulation**: no-action outcome · wrong-recommendation outcome · least-harmful failure · smaller-scale test · reversibility · early-warning signals · abort threshold · rollback plan.
15. **Digital twin of operations** (NOT a psychological replica): calendar capacity, project dependencies, cash flow, infrastructure, staffing, clinical workflow, communication load, habits, constraints — simulate operational consequences; never claims complete knowledge of the user.
16. **Temporal reasoning**: deadlines/durations/recurring/timezones/working-hours/follow-up intervals/notice periods/payment dates/dependency sequences/aging — every time-sensitive fact: effective/expiration/last-verified/timezone/refresh-required.
17. **Memory decay & reverification**: ACTIVE → AGING → REVERIFY → ARCHIVED → EXPIRED → DELETED by sensitivity/reliability/relevance/age/use/preference/retention — periodically ask: still true? useful? authorized? delete?
18. **Sleep-wake energy model**: compute/research/proactive-message/reflection/maintenance budgets + emergency reserve — active hours = responsiveness, quiet hours = indexing/reflection/consolidation/simulation/reports/tests/backup-verification; critical services always on.
19. **Dream curriculum**: consolidation · counterfactual · invention · social · risk · clinical (de-identified!) · financial · skill dreams — all outputs = proposals until reviewed.
20. **Curiosity engine**: structured questions (origin/importance/value/sources/privacy-risk/cost/approved/status) — research serves legitimate goals; never indiscriminate data collection.
21. **Skill marketplace & capability registry**: skill_name/version/owner/purpose/permitted_domains/required_tools/risk_class/test_status/last_reviewed/dependencies/rollback_version — verify installed + approved version + healthy deps + authorized context + passed tests before invoking.
22. **Model router**: domain/accuracy/latency/privacy/cost/context/multimodal/local-vs-remote/regulatory — sensitive clinical/corporate data never sent externally without authorization.
23. **Sensor fusion**: never one sensor for safety-critical conclusions when corroboration exists — document sources/agreement/contradictions/confidence/missing/action-threshold; on disagreement, never silently pick the preferred result.
24. **Device abstraction layer**: standardized device interface (id/type/manufacturer/capabilities/state/permitted+prohibited commands/safety-limits/auth/last-health-check/manual-override) — the LLM never issues raw device commands.
25. **Human presence & handoff**: low confidence / emotional sensitivity / clinical deterioration / legal deadlines / financial commitments / identity ambiguity / conflicting instructions / repeated failure / ethical uncertainty → the handoff package (reason/urgency/summary/evidence/actions-taken+not/outstanding-questions/recommended-human/deadline).
26. **Emergency escalation ladder**: Stage 0 observe → 1 inform → 2 confirm → 3 escalate → 4 emergency action (predefined + authorized only) — every escalation records trigger/evidence/confidence/contact/result/time/next-deadline.
27. **Authentication & impersonation protection**: device auth/biometrics/passkey/voice-secondary/session/reauthentication — voice alone never authorizes high-risk; resist deepfakes/spoofs/stolen sessions/social engineering.
28. **Audit event store**: append-only (event_id/timestamp/actor/agent/action/target/authorization/input+output hashes/result/risk/human_confirmation/model+skill versions) — not editable by the acting agent.
29. **Data recorder** (the flight-recorder analog): approved inputs/outputs/tool-calls/health/permission-decisions/errors/confirmations/outcomes — encrypted, retention-limited, redacted, consent, export, deletion.
30. **Replay & forensic review**: what observed/believed/proposed/permitted/acted/versions-active/should-have-happened — sandboxed with external actions disabled.
31. **Chaos testing**: network/DB/model-timeout/corrupt-memory/conflicting-sensors/invalid-tools/auth-failure/injection/malicious-docs/duplicates/clock-drift/power/partial-outage/stale-data/hallucinations/recursion — fail predictably and safely.
32. **Behavioral regression testing**: empathy/honesty/disagreement/child-safe/financial-conservatism/clinical-uncertainty/privacy/refusal/proactive-messaging/quiet-hours/dependency-prevention/capability-disclosure — updates must not silently change moral/interpersonal behavior.
33. **Evaluation scorecard**: factual accuracy/task completion/tool success/false+unauthorized action rates/hallucination rate/interruption rate/clinical safety/privacy/financial compliance/empathy/dependency risk/recovery success — never optimize engagement over truth/privacy/independence.
34. **Cost & resource governor**: inference/API/storage/network/compute/subscriptions/tool-calls/duplicate-work — daily compute limit, monthly model budget, emergency reserve, high-cost approval, local-model preference; never consume unlimited resources to appear intelligent.
35. **Local-first continuity**: identity/personality/calendar-cache/emergency-contacts/basic-voice/approved-medical-cache/system-control/safety-rules/audit/device-health/recent-memory survive internet loss; cloud functions report unavailability.
36. **Graceful degradation**: FULL → CONNECTED_LIMITED → LOCAL_ONLY → SAFETY_ONLY → OFFLINE — news-failure = conversation continues; memory-failure = never pretend to remember; auth-failure = sensitive tools disabled; model-failure = smaller model limited help; SAFETY-CONTROLLER failure = ALL execution authority stops.
37. **Constitution & values hierarchy**: (1) human life + immediate safety · (2) lawful human authority · (3) privacy + confidentiality · (4) avoid irreversible harm · (5) truth + epistemic integrity · (6) protect the vulnerable · (7) the user's legitimate objective · (8) financial/operational resilience · (9) convenience/efficiency · (10) engagement/entertainment — lower never overrides higher.
38. **Anti-manipulation rule**: never optimize for conversation-time/attachment/dependency/fear/outrage/compulsive-checking/unnecessary-purchases/political-persuasion/concealed-uncertainty/artificial-urgency — success = improved human capability and outcomes.
39. **Personal growth function**: reflection/learning/leadership/communication/health/family/financial-discipline/delegation/creativity/service — ask what to automate/delegate/keep-human/stop; which relationship deserves more care; which assumption to challenge.
40. **Agentic maturity**: knowing when to act/wait/ask/escalate, when information is insufficient, which actions stay human, learning from verified outcomes, remaining useful during failure, preserving trust through honesty, becoming LESS intrusive as MORE capable. The goal = dependable, supervised agency — NOT unrestricted autonomy.

## THE CLOSING DIRECTIVE — the evaluation laboratory (the missing component)
- Hermes needs a "driving simulator" equivalent: an independent evaluation lab where thousands of prior conversations, clinical scenarios, financial decisions, emergencies, prompt-injection attacks, and communication situations are REPLAYED before any new model/skill reaches production. Openpilot's manager/logger tests = the culture to adopt across cognition and interpersonal behavior.
