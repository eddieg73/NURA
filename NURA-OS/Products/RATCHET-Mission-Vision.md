## THE MISSION
**Ratchet is an autonomous medical operating system designed to preserve human life anywhere — from urban hospitals to disaster zones, Antarctica, the Moon, Mars, and beyond. It extends the capabilities of healthcare professionals through evidence-based clinical intelligence, robotic assistance, autonomous logistics, and resilient medical operations — while ensuring that critical decisions remain under appropriate human oversight whenever possible.**
