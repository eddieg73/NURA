# PROJECT RATCHET — AUTONOMOUS MEDICAL INTELLIGENCE & ROBOTIC CLINICAL SUPPORT (2026-08-04, founder canonical)

**For the Moon, Mars, and deep space — self-contained medical operations when Earth is unavailable, comms are delayed, supplies are limited, and the caregiver is not trained for the procedure in front of them.**

## 1. MISSION
Autonomous medical operations officer · clinical decision support · robotic procedure assistant · medical inventory manager · emergency instructor — for isolated environments. Supports: physicians outside their specialty · nurses/paramedics/engineers/scientists · crew medical officers with limited experience · NONMEDICAL personnel when no clinician remains · robotic examination when human access is unsafe · remote Earth specialists when comms permit · autonomous ops during blackouts/latency. (NASA's Autonomous Medical Operations work: exploration can't run on the LEO continuous-telemedicine model.)

## 2. THE CENTRAL DESIGN PRINCIPLE — two separable systems
**A. Ratchet Intelligence** (reasoning/knowledge/workflow/simulation/safety/documentation/mission management — runs on: habitat, Starship, rovers, tablet, AR headset, trauma case, humanoid body, hardened server).
**B. Ratchet Robotic Body** (carry equipment · position patients · retrieve meds · hold instruments · operate ultrasound · airway assist · compression · fracture stabilization · sterile fields · IV tubing · teleoperation · narrowly-authorized autonomous procedures).
**A failed knee actuator must not disable the medical brain; a corrupted medical model must not control unrestricted motor functions.**

## 5. INTERACTIVE PROCEDURE GUIDANCE (closed-loop instructor — NOT "insert at the 5th ICS")
Identify operator + competency → confirm patient/indication/consent/emergency authority → inventory tools → recommend the safest procedure → preview → verify positioning/anatomy (CV or US) → ONE step at a time → require confirmation (verbal/visual/sensor/clinician) → detect deviations → stop at hazard threshold → immediate rescue actions → document everything → postprocedure monitoring. **Perception-linked procedural control, not conversational AI.**

## 6. MEDICATION & PHARMACY INTELLIGENCE
Per drug: names · formulation · concentration · weight-based dosing · renal/hepatic adjustment · age/pregnancy · interactions · allergy cross-reactivity · route · infusion prep/compatibility · light/temp/radiation sensitivity · degradation · shelf-life extension evidence · repackaging · reconstitution · alternatives/substitutes · adverse-event response · toxicology/antidotes · conservation rules · formulary status · prescriber authority · dual-verification. **Must answer: "Out of ceftriaxone — best substitute?" · "Epinephrine 18 months expired — potency evidence?" · "Compound this tablet into a suspension?" · "Prioritize the refrigerated storage?" · "Courses remaining for six crew?" · "Degraded after thermal excursion?" · "Manufacture with the onboard pharmaceutical printer?"**

## 7. RESOURCE-AWARE MEDICINE
Every recommendation constrained by: clinical need × operator capability × available equipment/medication/consumables × power × oxygen × sterilization × mission duration × evacuation feasibility × risk to remaining crew. Distinguish: ideal Earth standard · best available mission treatment · conservation option · last resort · the point where treatment endangers the whole mission. **Ratchet = a medical logistics commander, not just a diagnostic assistant.**

## 8. THE AUTONOMY LADDER (levels 0-6)
0 Reference only · 1 Decision support · 2 Guided workflow · 3 Confirmed automation (robot prepares med after verification) · 4 Supervised robotic action (teleop suturing/probe) · 5 Conditional autonomy (validated task, clinician supervises) · **6 Emergency autonomy (only when delay would probably cause death and no qualified operator): activating emergency oxygen · calling/guiding CPR · automated compression · AED-directed defibrillation · mission-approved rescue med after DETERMINISTIC verification · direct pressure/tourniquet · fire/toxic/decompression protocols. Never unrestricted "autonomous medicine."**

## 9. PHYSICAL DESIGN
**Humanoid torso** (two high-dexterity arms · force/torque sensing · tactile fingertips · sterilizable surfaces · interchangeable hands · camera/depth) + **stable mobile base** (wheeled/tracked first — safer, lower power, better payload; bipedal later for ladders/terrain) + **detachable medical module** (US probe · digital stethoscope · otoscope/ophthalmoscope · dermoscope · ECG · vitals · thermal · lab reader · airway camera · instrument tracking · sterile attachments) + **environmental sensors** (O2/CO2/CO · pressure · radiation · temp/humidity · volatiles · dust). Tesla's Optimus = a mobility reference; Ratchet needs medical-grade force limits, sterile interfaces, traceable software, deterministic emergency behavior, fault-tolerant sensing — outside Optimus's general-purpose scope.

## 10. COMMUNICATION ARCHITECTURE (4 modes)

## 17. CLINICAL SAFETY ENGINE (every output separates)
Confirmed patient facts · unconfirmed observations · interpretation · ranked differential · must-not-miss · relative likelihood · clinical danger · recommended actions · required operator competence · remaining uncertainty · resource impact · reassessment interval · escalation/evacuation criteria. **Example:** Confirmed: pleuritic chest pain · SpO2 83% · hypotension · absent right lung sliding → Most likely: right tension pneumothorax → Must-not-miss: PE · tamponade · equipment hypoxia → Immediate: treat as tension PTX under emergency protocol → Operator: CMO preferred/paramedic acceptable/nonmedical only under Ratchet guidance → Resource impact: one decompression catheter + chest-tube kit + 6-12h O2.
