## THE PRACTICE OS SCOPE ("More than Weave")
### Core communication tools
- AI Voice Phone System: VoIP, call recording, missed-call text back, call pop-ups w/ patient info
- Two-Way Text Messaging: SMS, group texting, automated reminders + recall campaigns
- Online Scheduling: self-booking, waitlists, calendar sync, automated confirmations
- Patient Communication Hub: unified inbox (calls, messages)
- Reputation Management: review requests, monitor/respond Google/Facebook/RealSelf
- Payments: secure payments, deposits, plans, financing, memberships

### AI modules
- NURA AI Assistant (copilot: call summaries, notes, follow-ups, Q&A)
- Clinical Documentation: AI SOAP notes, treatment plans, eRx, labs, imaging, FHIR interop, eSign + eFax
- Surgical & Aesthetic Tools: before/after photos, consents, treatment plans, inventory tracking
- Revenue Cycle Management: auth, claims & denials, collections, revenue forecasting
- Analytics & Insights: ops, financials, AI performance, provider metrics

### Automation
AI lead nurturing · smart workflows (build once, automate) · task automation (70%+ admin reduction) · patient journeys · 24/7 availability
