**Founder directive: "I may take a salary, but I would like my compensation agreement like Elon Musk." Reference model: Tesla 2018 CEO Performance Award. DRAFT — board approval + licensed counsel review + Reg A disclosure required. ASC 718 expense treatment with Assurance.**

---

## 1. THE MODEL (Tesla 2018, translated)
Tesla's plan: $0 salary / $0 cash bonus · 100% at-risk stock options · 12 tranches · EACH tranche gates on a market-cap milestone ($50B→$650B) AND an operational milestone (revenue/EBITDA step) · 10-year term · all-or-nothing per tranche · 5-year post-vest holding.

**NURA translation (private Wyoming LLC):** modest-or-nominal salary (founder's choice) + **performance-vesting founder equity in 12 tranches**, each gated on BOTH a **valuation/raise milestone** AND an **operational KPI** — compensation ONLY when the company wins.

## 2. THE SALARY OPTION (founder's choice)
- **Option A (Musk-pure)**: $0 salary, $0 bonus — 100% at-risk equity
- **Option B (pragmatic)**: market base (e.g., $150-250k, board-set annually) + the same performance equity — salary covers living expenses only, never a windfall
- Recommendation: **Option B at the low end** — keeps founder cash-flow-safe while equity stays the entire upside. Board sets; documented in Reg A Exec Comp.

## 4. MECHANICS
- **Term**: 10 years from grant (Tesla model); tranches vest all-or-nothing; failed milestone = tranche lapses (no partial vest)
- **Hold**: 5-year post-exercise/vest holding (founder skin in the game)
- **Form**: performance-vesting membership units/options per WY LLC operating agreement → convert to common at corporate conversion (ties to the Reg A structure)
- **Accounting**: ASC 718 grant-date fair value expensed over the award — Assurance books this (the attorney×accounting joint workstream)
- **Governance**: approved by the Board (Atlas-chartered governance; Advisor consult); independent director concurrence recommended; founder recuses from own compensation vote
- **Disclosure**: full terms in the Reg A Offering Circular "Executive Compensation" + "Certain Relationships & Related Party Transactions" sections (the draft's §10/§11 — the SEC specialist's review scope)

## 5. WHY THIS IS OFFERING-FRIENDLY
Musk-style plans signal alignment (investors love them when milestones are real); the plan is a disclosed related-party item — Reg A requires disclosure, not prohibition; valuation milestones tie DIRECTLY to the offering/raise ladder (the founder's own capital stack as the yardstick); IP (the 22-claim package + continuation) is the plan's collateral per Assurance's software-dev capitalization line.
