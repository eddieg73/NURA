# NURA SALES PLAN — Dan Martell Strategy (audience-first, 2026-08-02)
Dan Martell's core rule: KNOW WHO YOUR AUDIENCE IS before you sell anything. Clarity of buyer > clarity of product.

## 1. THE AUDIENCE (ICP — one sentence each)
- **ICP-1 Solo Provider** (aesthetics/functional medicine): one owner-clinician, 50–150 pts/wk, drowning in notes + follow-ups, buys on ROI-to-time and simplicity. Buys solo (<$200/mo), self-serve, after a demo + trial.
- **ICP-2 Small Group Practice** (primary care/MA panel, 3–10 providers): admin staff + billing manager; buys on staffing savings + revenue (coding +11%, faster auth). Buying committee: owner + office manager; 30–90d cycle; wants references.
- **ICP-3 Med-Spa / Aesthetic Clinic**: high-margin services, before/after media needs; buys on patient experience + marketing engine (SOP-5/6/7) + bundle (CRM/imaging). Fast buying (demo-driven), price-tolerant.
- **ICP-4 Medicare Advantage Group (our anchor: Medisun/NURA Imaging)**: full-risk, RAF/HCC-sensitive; buys on RAF accuracy + care gaps + TCM/RPM revenue; compliance-heavy; long cycle; reference-led.
- **NOT our audience yet**: large hospital systems (Epic-native) — Phase 2 via P7 adapters; enterprises needing procurement cycles.

## 2. THE PROBLEM (why they buy)
Fragmentation: 6+ systems, cognitive switching, 2–3h of documentation daily, missed revenue (gaps, denials, RAF), staffing burnout. The old pitch: "AI scribe." The NURA pitch: **"one app that runs your practice — and Hermes, the operator, is baked in."** Nobody else ships the operator with the software.

## 3. THE OFFER (pricing per Synthetic-Clinician plan)
Tier1 Solo $100 · Tier2 Group $125 · Tier3 Health System $150 per provider/mo · add-ons: analytics +$25, custom AI +$50, pro services $10–50K. Bundle framing: CRM + EMR + RIS/PACS + Hermes agent layer INCLUDED (no line-item selling of modules — modules are the bundle).

## 4. THE FUNNEL (feed from the media engine)
1. **Top**: NURA Media Engine (Reel) — SOP-5 blog-to-reel, SOP-6 testimonial/before-after syndication (@nuratech.ai + @garrido.eddie) with UTM → Perfex leads. Weekly blog (peptides/HRT/GLP-1) positions expertise.
2. **Middle**: value content → free demo ("see your practice run itself in 15 min") → **7-day trial with Hermes running a real workflow** (scheduling + recall + one scribe note). No-touch demo = the differentiator: prospects SEE the agents work.
3. **Close**: reference-led (NURA Imaging hub as flagship case study), ROI one-pager per ICP (time saved × provider rate; coding +11%; denials ↓).
4. **Expand**: module adoption (RPM/CCM enrollment → +revenue per patient), group expansion, multi-location.

## 5. OBJECTION HANDLING
- "We're on Epic" → adapter plug-in (FHIR/SMART) — that's the point of ONE app; onboarding = adapter, not replacement.
- "HIPAA?" → local-first/PHI-stays-local architecture, BAAs, audit logs, provider-review gates, MQSA/ACR for imaging bundle.
- "AI hallucination risk" → evidence-grounded (FDA/PubMed/CDC/OpenEvidence citations), clinician review, deterministic rules for coding/RAF.
- "Another vendor?" → NURA replaces the 6 vendors, not adds to them (ROI math: 6 subscriptions → 1).
- "Cost" → $100–150/provider vs DAX $500–1,200; free-tier AI lanes keep unit costs low.

## 6. SALES MOTION (per ICP)
ICP-1/3: demo-led, self-serve trial, NPS>70 target, word-of-mouth via media engine.
ICP-2/4: consultative — pilot (one provider/one workflow) → expand; success metrics (doc time, chart closure, coding lift, gap closure) reported monthly by Hermes; champion = office manager/CMO.
All: **sales collateral generated by Iris (CMO) via the board**; pipeline tracked in NURA CRM (Perfex); forecasts weekly in CEO scrum.

## 7. TEAM (who sells)
Iris (CMO) owns messaging + funnel · Atlas (CEO) owns pipeline/forecasts · Nova (CS) owns onboarding/success · Reel owns content engine · Hermes owns demo automation + trial execution + case-study data. Sales = human-led, Hermes-supported (demos, ROI docs, follow-ups via Twilio/email).
