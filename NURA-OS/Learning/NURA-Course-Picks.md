# The NURA course picks — the curated (2026-08-19)

From the 366-course scrape. The picks fill the real gaps: the clinical ML engineering, the vision theory, the aviation systems, the epidemiology. Everything else in the index is reference; these are the intake.

## The picks (the ranked)

1. **Machine Learning for Healthcare** — David Sontag, Peter Szolovits, MIT (YouTube playlist)
   Why: the single most direct gap — the clinical ML methodology (the labels, the validation, the deployment ethics) that the radiology AI engine and the Med42 fine-tunes need. The MIT's clinical ML curriculum is the exact discipline the NURA's vision engine must follow.

2. **Convolutional Neural Networks** — Andrew Ng
   Why: the imaging lane's foundation. The radiology AI (the chest X-ray, the mammo, the CT nodule detection) is CNN work; this is the formal depth behind the TorchXRayVision usage.

3. **Artificial Intelligence: Machine Learning (CS229)** — Andrew Ng, Stanford
   Why: the backbone math/ML the coding agents lean on. The agents operate the tools today; CS229 is the theory that makes the fine-tuning and the evaluation principled instead of cargo-cult.

4. **Deep Learning** — Nando de Freitas, Oxford
   Why: the deeper theory (the attention, the sequence models) behind the LLM fine-tunes and the MCP agent design.

5. **Epidemiology and Control of Infectious Diseases** — Tomas Aragon, UC Berkeley
   Why: the EMS/public-health lane. The mesh, the disaster response, the field medicine — the epidemiological frame is the missing layer in the EMS doctrine.

6. **Aircraft Systems Engineering** — MIT OCW
   Why: the pilot's systems depth. The E6B lane and the turboprop/Vision Jet ambitions need the systems-level knowledge (the avionics, the hydraulics, the electrical).

7. **Foundations of Understanding and Combating Cancer** — Harold Varmus, Cornell Medicine
   Why: the oncology adjacent — the imaging corpus is lung-cancer heavy (the LIDC/LUNA), and the screening programs (the Lung-RADS) need the disease biology behind the guidelines.
