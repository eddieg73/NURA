## Operations
Closed loop: referral intake → eligibility/auth → scheduling → reminders/prep → exam + tech QA → read → report delivery → billing/denials. TAT: stat <4h, routine 24–48h. Multilingual (EN/ES/Creole) access — 3 agents (one per language). Cluster MRI/CT blocks; no-show reduction.

## Technology (matches our stack)
Orthanc PACS + ThaiRIS (scheduling/orders) + OHIF viewer · eMedical EMR integration · Supabase · Zapier · GHL · AI-assisted multilingual patient engagement. Security: encryption, RBAC, logging, secure remote read, patching, backups, downtime recovery. Enterprise-grade controls, not "low-cost stack" positioning.

## Compliance roadmap (CRITICAL)
- **ACR accreditation**: stepwise (modality finalization → physicist testing → QC logs → applications → phantoms/samples → deficiencies → ongoing audit). MRI/CT/mammo modality-specific.
- **MQSA**: mammography launch contingent on full MQSA readiness (FDA-approved accreditor, annual inspections, QA program, annual physicist surveys).
- **AHCA**: health-care-clinic framework — DBA/service-line/expansion change filings (Form 3110-0013).
- **DOH radiation control**: equipment registration, radiation safety, personnel standards; PET-CT licensure (Phase 2).
- **Image Wisely / Image Gently**: dose protocols, physicist involvement, shielding, machine registration, tech competency.
- **Fraud & abuse**: AKS + Stark — all ownership/compensation/medical-director/referral arrangements at FMV, commercially reasonable, NOT tied to referral volume.

## GTM
Internal referrals + external (ortho, pain, neuro, OB/GYN, cardio) + patient acquisition (women's health/preventive, online scheduling, localized SEO, multilingual campaigns). Partnerships within Stark/AKS guardrails.

## Risks & mitigations
Reimbursement compression · slow ramp · credentialing delays · labor inflation · downtime · compliance failure → Phase-1 capital discipline, auth workflow, cross-training, service agreements, formal compliance oversight. **Sequencing is the #1 mitigation.**
