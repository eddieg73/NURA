# TECHNICAL PRODUCT MANAGER & AGILE DELIVERY LEAD — the role spec (2026-08-04)

**Position:** Technical Product Manager & Agile Delivery Lead · **Funnel:** the AI hiring manager · **Signer:** the founder

## 1. MISSION
Turn the founder's intent and the role specs into a shipping machine: the roadmap, the backlog, the sprints, the delivery cadence, and the cross-functional coordination (engineering, clinical, security, ops) — with the doctrine: **the specs are the contract, the exams are the gates, and the founder's priorities are the compass.**

## 2. CORE OWNERSHIP
**Product:** the roadmap (from the founder's priorities: the lighthouse first, the Sidecar, the SaaS-ification) · PRDs that translate the role specs into buildable epics · feature prioritization (RICE-class, clinical safety weighted) · acceptance criteria (testable, from the Clinical Director's discipline) · release planning.
**Delivery:** sprint cadence · the kanban/board (Paperclip integration) · dependency management (the integration map) · blockers resolution · velocity + burndown honesty · the Definition of Done (the QA release gates) · the demo cadence (founder reviews).
**Coordination:** the bridge between the Clinical Director and engineering · the funnels (the hiring manager's handoff) · vendor/partner milestones (Twilio/Tavus/OpenEvidence-class) · the executive status (one-page, honest, data-first).

## 3. REQUIRED STACK & EXPERIENCE
Jira/Linear-class · GitHub Projects · roadmap tools · analytics (product metrics) · API/technical literacy (the candidate can read a spec and challenge an estimate) · A/B and metrics thinking.
**Experience:** 5+ yrs technical product/delivery · 3+ yrs healthcare/regulated software (preferred) · shipped products end-to-end · worked with clinical stakeholders · the ability to say no with evidence · the calm to run a release with 12 moving parts.

## 4. RECOMMENDED CERTIFICATIONS
CSPO/PSPO · SAFe (or equivalent scaling) · PMP · Lean Six Sigma · healthcare product training. Certs ≠ ability — the track record decides.

## 5. THE PRACTICAL EXAM (the gate)
Given the founder's one-paragraph intent ("get the Medisun lighthouse live with the Sidecar workflow, then SaaS-ify"), deliver: the 90-day roadmap with sequenced epics and dependencies, the sprint-1 backlog with testable acceptance criteria (clinical-safety gates included), the risk register (top 10 with mitigations), the resource plan (who does what across the 13 roles + agents), and the one-page executive status format. **Evaluation: prioritization quality, dependency thinking, criteria testability, honesty about uncertainty.**

## 6. FIRST 90-DAY DELIVERABLES
**1-30:** the master roadmap (lighthouse → Sidecar → SaaS) · the backlog structure · the delivery cadence · the Definition of Done (with the QA gates) · the status one-pager.
**31-60:** sprint execution (the first clinical pilot milestones) · the dependency map live · the risk register · the vendor milestone tracking.
**61-90:** the pilot release coordination · the retrospective + the process deltas · the roadmap v2 with the measured progress · the executive dashboards.

## 7. KPIs
Milestone delivery rate (vs plan) · sprint velocity + predictability · cycle time (backlog → shipped) · blocked-item count · scope-change discipline · roadmap accuracy · clinical-pilot outcomes · team satisfaction. **Targets: silent slips = 0 (every slip communicated early) · ship dates with the safety gates honored = 100%.**

## 8. THE JD SUMMARY
The Technical PM & Agile Delivery Lead converts the founder's vision and the specs' contracts into shipped, gated, honest delivery — the person who makes sure "the company builds itself on paper before it builds in code" stays true in practice.
