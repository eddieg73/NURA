# HEALTHCARE UI/UX & HUMAN-FACTORS DESIGNER — the role spec (2026-08-04)

**Position:** Healthcare UI/UX & Human-Factors Designer · **Funnel:** the AI hiring manager · **Signer:** the founder

## 1. MISSION
Make NURA feel like medicine, not software: the clinician's cockpit (mobile + web + dashboard) designed around human factors — reduced documentation time, zero wrong-patient ambiguity, visible criticals, and adoption that comes from trust. **The doctrine: the interface is a safety device.**

## 2. CORE OWNERSHIP
**Clinical UX:** the patient banner · the chart navigation · the encounter workspace · the work queues · the escalation views (Levels 1-5) · the AI-draft distinction (never looks final) · the sync states (never looks complete when transmitted) · med-rec and prescribing flows · results + imaging views.
**Human factors:** cognitive-load reduction · alert-fatigue management · the 8 usability principles from the Clinical Director's spec (criticals visible · destructive confirmed · similar patients distinguishable · uncertainty visible · no implied success) · error-proofing (the wrong-patient visual anchors) · the accessibility baseline (WCAG 2.2 AA).
**Design system:** the NURA brand (safety orange/navy/white) · component library (the Web Architect's spec's list) · tokens · patterns · the design-to-code handoff (Storybook/Figma-class) · clinical alert hierarchy (info → emergency, color never alone).
**Research:** clinician interviews · workflow shadowing · usability testing (the lighthouse providers) · the adoption metrics · iterative refinement with the Clinical Director.

## 3. REQUIRED STACK & EXPERIENCE
Figma (or equivalent) · design systems · prototyping · usability testing · accessibility (WCAG, a11y audits) · design tokens · Storybook/Framer-class handoff · analytics-informed iteration.
**Experience:** 5+ yrs product design · 3+ yrs healthcare/clinical UX (EMR/EHR-class) · demonstrated safety-critical interface work (aviation/medical precedents valued) · the ability to argue with clinicians in their language and engineers in theirs.

## 4. RECOMMENDED CREDENTIALS
NN/g UX certification · IAAP accessibility certifications · CHFP (human factors professional) where applicable · healthcare-specific UX training. Certs ≠ ability — the portfolio decides.

## 5. THE PRACTICAL EXAM (the gate)
Redesign one critical clinical flow (the abnormal-result review or the med-rec) for the NURA dashboard: the current-state critique, the proposed flow with the human-factors rationale (what can go wrong and how the design prevents it), the accessibility audit, the prototype, and the usability-test plan with the pass criteria (e.g., time-to-acknowledge a critical result, error rate on patient selection). **Evaluation: safety thinking, clinical fluency, craft, accessibility, measurable UX targets.**

## 6. FIRST 90-DAY DELIVERABLES
**1-30:** the design system v1 (tokens + core components + the alert hierarchy) · the patient banner spec · the wrong-patient visual standards.
**31-60:** the encounter workspace v1 · the work-queue designs · the escalation UX (5 levels) · the AI-draft visual language · the sync-state language.
**61-90:** the med-rec + results redesigns · usability testing with the lighthouse providers · the accessibility pass · the adoption metrics baseline.

## 7. KPIs
Documentation time per encounter · clicks per task · wrong-patient ambiguity events · critical-alert acknowledgment time · alert-fatigue (dismissal rates) · usability test pass rates · provider satisfaction (the CSAT) · accessibility compliance · adoption/retention. **Targets: wrong-patient ambiguity = 0 · missed criticals = 0 · implied-success states = 0.**

## 8. THE JD SUMMARY
The Healthcare UI/UX & Human-Factors Designer makes NURA's safety promises visible and its workflows effortless: an interface clinicians trust at 3am with a critical result on screen. The role requires design mastery, clinical empathy, and the rigor to prove the design works before it ships.
