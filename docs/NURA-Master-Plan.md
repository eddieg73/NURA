## 1. The Mission (one line)
> **Build the first autonomous healthcare intelligence that gives clinicians their life back — then put it in the physical world, and finally in the mind of the operator. Own the system, not the product. See the real world. Compound toward ASI.**

## 2. The Problem (grounded)
Physicians spend **~1.77 h/day documenting outside office hours → ~125M physician-hours/year** (JAMA); **~2h documentation per 1h patient care** (JMIR/PMC); AMA **5+ h in the EHR per 8h with patients**. Burnout, time lost from life, unclaimed revenue, missed risk-adjustment.

## 3. The Product / Stack
One **Agent-OS** (Hermes) across **CRM · EMR · ERP · RIS · PACS · labs · diagnostics** + **MA risk/HCC coding** + **physical (drones, robots)**. **UX = moat:** a Doximity-style **ambient/voice-first, no-UI screen** — the clinician never types a form; **touch is the fallback, not the primary.** Sell the fastest path to "done."

## 4. The Customer + GTM (Hormozi)
Sell to the **clinical champion** in a health system. **Value equation** `(dream outcome × likelihood) ÷ (time × effort)` → win on time + reliability. **Offer:** *"Every clinician home by 6, fully documented and billed."* **Cost-per-outcome**, risk-reversed trial, give-first pilot.

## 5. The Market (grounded)
- Ambient documentation **~$600M in 2025 (+2.4×)** + coding ~$450M. Leaders: Abridge ($100M ARR, $5.3B), Nuance DAX (33%, 77% of US hospitals), Ambience ($1.25B), OpenEvidence (40% of US physicians, $6.5B), Epic (~40% EHR).
- **Agentic healthcare AI $0.71B → $5.78B by 2031 (~42% CAGR)**; autonomous documentation fastest-growing (→ ~$27B by 2035).
- **MA risk-adjustment/HCC coding** = high-margin moat (CMS flags ~$17B overpayments; V28/RADV).
- **Physical:** Zipline ($7.6B, 2.5M+ deliveries), ArcherFRS whole-blood <3 min, Diligent Moxi (1M+ deliveries).
- **TAM:** docs + back-office RCM ≈ 60% of healthcare IT spend ≈ **$38B** intelligence layer. *Honest: converging/invented category — directional, not investment-grade.*

## 6. Business Model
Outcomes-based SaaS (per-hour / per-document / per-clean-claim), **$0 sovereign cost floor** → **margin as a moat.** MA risk-coding = high-margin wedge. Physical = managed-service + per-delivery.

## 7. Phased Roadmap (self-funding)
1. **Phase 1 — intelligent wedge:** autonomous doc + coding for one specialty, ambient/no-UI. *Funds itself.*
2. **Phase 2 — integrated stack:** one Agent-OS over CRM/EMR/ERP/RIS/PACS/labs/dx + MA risk-coding.
3. **Phase 3 — occupy the physical world:** drones + robots + humanoids. *"You must see the real world."* → data flywheel.
4. **Phase 4 — the brain link:** augment the operator (BCI frontier).

## 8. The Moat
The **single end-to-end autonomous loop** (ambient intake → coding → imaging → drone/robot delivery) **no one owns** + proprietary clinical data + deep integrations + regulatory head-start + **$0 sovereign cost floor**.

## 9. The Org (SpaceX × Tesla × AI)
One engine (Agent-OS) → divisions as rockets (Clinical, Revenue/Admin, Aero, Robotics) → shared factory (memory + COP + iteration). CTO owns stack, CAO owns intelligence, division VPs own rockets. Cross-division flywheel → AGI/ASI.

## 10. Financing
Self-funding Phase 1 → capital-efficient scale. **Optional YC** as distribution + capital, but the **ecosystem stays sovereign** (model swappable, system is ours).

## 11. Competitive Positioning — Doximity vs NURA
> **Doximity is the physician's network (the LinkedIn of medicine) — an attention layer on top. NURA is the physician's autonomous operating system — the AI that does the work AND the backend (CRM · EMR · ERP · RIS · PACS) that stores the record.**

**Doximity (NYSE: DOCS, 10-K):** FY2025 **$570.4M (+20%), 89.5% GAAP gross margin, ~39% net margin, ~3M members / >85% of US physicians.** Three subscription businesses monetizing the *network*, not clinical data: **Marketing** (pharma sponsorship — core), **Hiring** (recruiting), **Workflow** (Dialer telehealth, on-call, digital fax, Doximity GPT). Moat = network + physician identity → engagement → pharma advertising. Thin AI assistant.

| | Doximity | NURA |
|---|---|---|
| Layer | Top-of-funnel network / attention | Full-stack intelligence + record |
| Owns | Physician identity + pharma-ad relationships | **The clinical data (EMR/RIS/PACS) + the AI that does the work** |
| AI | Thin assistant (Doximity GPT) | **Autonomous operator** |
| Monetization | Pharma advertising | **Outcomes (documentation/coding/work done) + data** |

**One-liner:** *"Doximity is the physician's social/professional network. NURA is the physician's autonomous operating system that owns and operates the record."*
