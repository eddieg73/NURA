Both ZOLL candidates — X Series and Propaq M — are rated to 15,000 ft. The HAMILTON-T1 is rated to 25,000 ft.

A naive integration takes min() = 15,000 ft, so ARES would be less capable than the ventilator it replaces. Rotary-wing mountain CASEVAC regularly exceeds 15,000 ft.

Resolution: re-qualify the acquisition front-end to 25,000 ft, or accept and document a 15,000 ft ceiling as a deliberate operational limitation.
What the Propaq M buys us that the X Series doesn't:
• USAF ATL + US Army USAARL airworthiness certification under JECETS — ACM for rotary wing, Safe-to-fly for fixed wing
• 3.9 kg vs 5.3 kg · 10.6 L vs 12.0 L · 7.5 h on 73 Wh
• NVG-friendly display mode
• Full Masimo rainbow SET including SpHb, SpOC, PI, PVI

Also caught: EtCO₂ reads low at altitude per Dalton's law — altitude compensation must be built into both the vent and the acquisition path, not bolted on.
