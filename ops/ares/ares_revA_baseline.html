02 — BAROMETRIC ENVIRONMENT MANAGER ▲ NEW · EVIDENCE-BACKED

promoted from "nice idea" to a validation-critical subsystem by the peer-reviewed data
The T1's 25,000 ft figure is an ENVIRONMENTAL RATING — not a volume-accuracy guarantee
Altitude-chamber study (PMID 25159349), sea level / 8,000 / 16,000 / 22,000 ft, Impact 731 · Hamilton T1 · CareFusion Revel:
• T1 delivered set VT within 10% only at 8,000 ft
• Above that, delivered VT exceeded set VT
• "Only the 731 actively accounts for changes in barometric pressure to maintain the set VT at all tested altitudes."
• "Altitude compensation is an active software algorithm."

Hamilton's own alarm table lists "Performance limited by high altitude" — the manufacturer acknowledges it. So a 25,000 ft rating buys survivability, not accuracy.
One manager drives every correction from a single ambient-pressure source: ventilator flow & volume · capnography (Dalton) · O₂ partial-pressure interpretation · gas density · membrane-lung sweep · concentrator output · cooling capacity · alarm thresholds. Handled per-subsystem it drifts; handled centrally it is one validated curve.
Required test protocol: altitude chamber, ±VT accuracy at sea level / 8k / 16k / 22k / 25k ft, adult and paediatric, 0 & 20 cmH₂O PEEP, FiO₂ 0.21 and 1.0 — with acceptance criteria stated up front. This is a proposal-strengthening glide path DARPA requires and our competitors will likely lack.

Honest reading — the stretch does not close
Gates 1 + 2 total 13.20 kg, already 0.80 kg past the 14 kg implicit budget inside a 15 kg chassis — before any structure. A 2.60 kg structural allowance is not optional for a MIL-STD sealed, litter-clamped device.

Recommendation: plan the baseline at 16–17 kg and carry ≤15 kg as an aspiration contingent on Gate 1 landing low. Stating 15 kg as a committed spec invites a reviewer to price a device that cannot be built.

Outside the gates (mission consumables, never hidden in device weight): O₂ / LOX, blood products, drugs, mission batteries.

Why the defib position is right: internalising defibrillation would drag high-voltage isolation, paddle/pad interfaces, capacitor charging, waveform validation and a separate EMC campaign into the first DARPA build — schedule and regulatory cost with no scored benefit.
Where the volume and mass savings actually come from
Not from three modules sharing a lid — from one instance of each shared thing: one barometric sensor, one SpO₂ channel, one EtCO₂ channel, one temperature system, one clock, one comms modem, one battery controller, one display, one alarm engine, one audit log. That discipline is the −2.60 kg and it is also what makes the device one machine in the DARPA sense rather than a system of systems.
