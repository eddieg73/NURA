03 — SINGLE-ACCESS FLUIDIC CIRCUIT

every therapy injected upstream of return — the membrane never alters a dose

04 — THE FINDING THE BLUEPRINT MISSED — mass is dominated by gas & power

the blueprint budgeted volumes (2,880 L / 8,640 L of O₂). It never budgeted kilograms.
Conclusion: ARES is not a backpack. It is a litter-mounted device with a resupply tail; the M9 form factor is achievable only for 2–4 hour hops. Either LOX, or the oxygen logistics — not the AI — decide the program.
Method & assumptions. Free gas = water-volume(L) × pressure(bar). Steel @200 bar ≈ 1.0 kg per L water volume; composite @300 bar ≈ 1.45 kg/L. LOX 1.141 kg/L, 860 L gas per L, +1.2 kg dewar. Battery 250 Wh/kg pack, 85% usable. Portable concentrator ≈ 90% O₂ at ~1 L/min per ~90–120 W. Mission envelope 2 L/min sweep, 24 h. All masses are first-order planning estimates at concept fidelity — not device measurements.
